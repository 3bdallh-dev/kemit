Our Mission

The Egyptian Museum stands as the world's greatest repository of Pharaonic antiquities. Since opening our doors in 1902, we have been dedicated to preserving, researching, and sharing the treasures of ancient Egypt with the world.

Our mission extends beyond exhibition. We conduct ongoing archaeological research, conservation efforts, and educational programs that bring the wonders of the Nile to new generations. Every artifact in our collection is a bridge between the past and the future.

A Century of Discovery

1902

Foundation

Designed by French architect Marcel Dourgnon, the museum opens in Tahrir Square to house Egypt's growing collection of antiquities.

1922

Tutankhamun

Howard Carter's discovery fills the museum with the boy king's treasures, drawing global attention to Egyptology.

2026

Today

Over 120,000 artifacts, 27 royal mummies, and millions of visitors annually. The museum continues to inspire wonder.
