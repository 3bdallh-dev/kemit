Colossal statue of Hatshepsut from her mortuary temple

  • Dynasty: 18th Dynasty
  • Period: New Kingdom
  • Reign: c. 1478–1458 BCE
  • Material: Limestone, Granite, Sandstone
  • Found in: Deir el-Bahari, Luxor
Description

Hatshepsut was one of the few female pharaohs to rule Egypt. She initially served as regent for her stepson Thutmose III but eventually assumed the full titles and regalia of a pharaoh. Her reign was marked by peace, prosperity, and magnificent building projects including her terraced temple at Deir el-Bahari.

Did You Know?

Hatshepsut often wore a false beard and masculine attire in official depictions to assert her authority as pharaoh!
